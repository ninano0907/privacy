EASY CUBE / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Your settings stay on your device. Apple handles Game Center and purchases. Here is how those features use information.

Effective / last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Who operates Easy Cube

Easy Cube is operated by Jinwon Kim (김진원). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Privacy contact: prometheusdev0916@gmail.com

2. Information handled by the app

The app does not require a separate developer-managed account. The current app contains no advertising SDK, advertising identifier collection, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. The developer does not operate a separate server to receive your gameplay or control settings.

Information Purpose and handling
Control positions, sizes and angles; rear-view layout; animation speed; selected puzzle Stored in the app’s local device storage to remember your preferences.
Game Center player identifier and pending best-time records Used to associate a pending score with the correct player. Pending times are stored locally and submitted to Apple Game Center after authentication.
Purchase entitlement Apple StoreKit verifies ownership of the non-consumable cube unlock product. The app uses the verified result to unlock puzzles.

3. Game Center

The app requests Game Center authentication on launch. You may continue playing if authentication is cancelled or fails. When a timed solve is completed, the app submits the puzzle’s leaderboard identifier and completion time to Apple Game Center using the authenticated player.

Game Center may show your player profile, nickname, rank and score to others according to Apple’s service settings. The developer does not receive your Apple Account password. Manage your Game Center profile and privacy in Apple’s settings.

4. In-app purchases

Apple processes payment, purchase history and restoration. The product identifier is com.nano.easycube.unlock_cube. The app checks verified current entitlements on launch and before opening a paid puzzle. You may also restore purchases from the purchase screen.

The developer does not receive your card number or other payment credentials. App Store Connect may provide the developer with sales, transaction and refund reports under Apple’s terms.

5. Sharing and Apple services

The app does not sell personal information or share it with advertising networks. Game Center scores and purchase verification are handled through Apple’s services for those features. Apple handles that information under its own privacy terms.

See Apple’s Privacy Policy, Apple Games & Privacy, and App Store & Privacy.

6. Retention and deletion

App preferences and any pending score records remain in local storage until removed or the app’s local data is deleted. A pending score is removed from the local queue after successful submission; other pending records may remain for later retry. Removing the app can remove its local data, subject to device backup and restore behavior.

Deleting the app does not delete Apple’s purchase history or Game Center records. Manage those records and any applicable deletion requests through Apple. The developer cannot directly delete information held in your Apple Account.

7. Your choices

You may play the free 3×3 without purchasing additional puzzles. You may decline Game Center sign-in and continue playing. To manage Game Center access and privacy, use your device’s Apple settings. Contact the developer with questions or requests about information the developer holds.

8. Email inquiries

If you email the developer, your email address and any information you choose to include are used to respond to and resolve your inquiry. Please do not send Apple Account passwords or payment card details. Inquiry information is retained only as needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted when no longer needed.

9. Children and security

The app does not ask for age, contact details, precise location, photos, camera or microphone access for gameplay. Apple may apply its own age and parental controls to Game Center and purchases. The app relies on Apple’s verified purchase results and Game Center authentication rather than collecting account credentials itself.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features or information practices change. The updated date will appear on this page.
