Please do not send passwords, your parental PIN, card numbers or a child’s name or date of birth. Hide personal information in any screenshots you attach.

The app will not open or stops responding

Close and reopen the app, then check the App Store for updates. If the issue continues, restart your device. Contact us with the details listed above if the problem recurs.

Game records are stored on your device. Deleting the app may erase your progress, so take care before deleting it to troubleshoot.

I paid, but paid stages are still locked

Check your internet connection and make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Pass the app’s parental gate, then select “Restore Purchases” on the purchase screen. If you have already paid, try restoring before making another purchase.

If approval is pending, wait for it to complete. If the problem continues, tell us what happened and share any error message without sending payment account passwords or card information.

Does restoring purchases restore game progress?

Game progress, stars, growth scores and preferences are stored on your device. The app does not offer account-based progress syncing. Restoring purchases restores access to paid content; it does not restore game records.

I forgot my parental PIN

In “Parent Settings,” choose the option to delete the parental PIN, or choose the forgotten-PIN option on the PIN entry screen. Complete the parental verification question shown to delete the PIN. You can then set a new PIN.

How do I change play time or sound?

You can manage the daily play time limit and sound effects in “Parent Settings.” Changing the time limit may require parental verification.

Privacy and deleting data

See our Privacy Policy for information about saved data and deletion. Deleting the app removes local game records and preferences, but does not delete Apple’s purchase history.
