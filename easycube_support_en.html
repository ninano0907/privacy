EASY CUBE

Six puzzles.
One intuitive way to play.

From the familiar 3×3 to Megaminx. Drag a joystick to turn a layer, explore a new angle, and find your next challenge.

How to playExplore six puzzles

Actual app screen · iPhone and iPad · English and Korean

Simple controls. Plenty to explore.

01

Drag to turn

Drag the joystick beside a layer along its track. Horizontal and diagonal controls use the same simple gesture.

02

Explore every angle

Use separate controls to rotate the whole cube. Check the small rear view to see hidden faces.

03

Make it yours

Adjust control positions and sizes, and change the angles of layer-turning joysticks. Move and resize the rear view, too.

The 3×3 cube is free. A single non-consumable in-app purchase permanently unlocks the other five puzzles. Restore your purchase from the purchase screen using the same Apple Account.

Practice at your pace. Challenge your best.

Adjust the rotation animation speed, shuffle your puzzle, or reset it to its solved state. Use Timed Solve to measure your solve time and explore all six Game Center leaderboards.

Game Center requires an Apple Account and an internet connection. Additional puzzle purchases require iOS/iPadOS 15 or later in the current app.
