Help and frequently asked questions

Lucky Ticket is a simulated scratch-card game for entertainment only. All tickets, coins, and prizes are virtual items and have no real-world monetary value.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. I did not receive my rewarded tickets.

Please check your internet connection and try again later. Rewards are provided only after the advertisement has been completed successfully.

2. Can I withdraw or exchange virtual coins?

No. Virtual coins, tickets, and prizes cannot be withdrawn, transferred, sold, or exchanged for cash, gift cards, physical goods, or anything of real-world value.

3. Is this a real lottery or gambling service?

No. Lucky Ticket does not sell real lottery tickets or provide real-money gambling. It is a simulated game created solely for entertainment.

4. Where is my game progress stored?

Your ticket inventory, virtual coin balance, and gameplay statistics are stored locally on your device. Removing the app may permanently delete this data.
