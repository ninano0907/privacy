Privacy Policy

Brain Sprouts · Effective date: November 6, 2026

Game records and settings stay on your device. The app uses no advertising or user tracking. In-app purchases are processed through Apple.

1. Scope and operator

This Privacy Policy applies to the Brain Sprouts (두뇌쑥쑥) iOS app. The operator is [Operator name]. The app does not require registration or a separate user account.

2. Information processed on your device and its purposes

  • Game records: Game stages, stars, completion records, elapsed time, mistakes and move counts are saved to show progress and growth screens. Growth scores are game rewards, not health or developmental assessments.
  • Play time and preferences: Daily usage time, time limits, warning status, selected character and sound preferences are saved to provide parental controls and app settings.
  • Parental PIN: The app stores a salt and hash for verification, PIN length, failed attempt counts and temporary lockout time. It does not store the PIN in plain text. These values protect parental settings.
  • Purchase status: The app saves whether paid stages are unlocked to provide access to purchased content.

This information is processed in local app storage and is not sent to the operator’s servers. The current version does not request names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or advertising identifiers. If a birth year was saved by an earlier version, the app converts it to a character preference and deletes the birth-year value.

3. Apple in-app purchases

Payments for paid content and purchase restoration are handled through the Apple App Store. The app processes product identifiers, transaction identifiers, receipts or transaction verification data, and purchase status supplied by Apple on your device to provide content and complete transactions. The app does not receive card numbers or payment account passwords, send transaction information to the operator’s servers, or separately save receipts in app preferences.

Apple processes and retains payment and purchase records under its own policies. See the Apple Privacy Policy for details.

4. Advertising, analytics and sharing

The current app does not use advertising, user tracking, third-party usage analytics or a separate crash reporting service. The operator does not sell or share local game records or preferences with third parties. Apple’s policies apply to the payment processing described above.

5. Children’s privacy

The app provides puzzle and memory games that children can use. It does not ask children to submit personal information or create an account. Game records and parental settings are processed on the device. Purchase and restore features are accessed through an in-app parental gate. Parents and guardians can manage play time in settings.

When contacting us, parents and guardians should avoid sending unnecessary personal information about a child, such as their name or date of birth.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally saved information is retained while needed to provide app features. Deleting the app removes its app data from that device. The iOS “Offload App” feature may retain data; choose “Delete App” to remove it. App data may be included in device or iCloud backups and may reappear when a backup is restored. You can manage backups through your Apple device and iCloud settings.

Deleting the app does not delete Apple’s purchase records. Restoring purchases may reactivate paid content. The operator cannot remotely access or delete local records on your behalf.

7. User and parental rights and safeguards

Users and parents or guardians can view records in the app, change available preferences, or delete the app to remove local data. You may contact us below to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing of personal information. For local information that the operator does not hold, we can explain how to manage it on your device rather than perform remote actions.

App data is kept within the iOS app storage area, and the parental PIN is stored as a salted hash. Please also protect your device lock and access to backups.

8. Support inquiries

If you email us, the operator processes your reply email address and the contents of your message to review and respond to your inquiry. You do not need to provide a child’s personal information. Inquiry information is deleted without undue delay once the response and follow-up are complete, unless retention is required by law, in which case it is deleted after that period. The policies of the email services used apply to email delivery and storage.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if app features or information practices change. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate means, such as an in-app notice.
